# 🧾 MUNIM – Your Voice-Powered Digital Khata

**Speak. Record. Track. Simplify.**

MUNIM is an AI-powered, voice-first digital ledger designed to make everyday financial record-keeping easier for small shopkeepers and local businesses.

Instead of manually entering every transaction, shopkeepers can simply speak naturally in their preferred language. MUNIM understands their voice, extracts important transaction details, and converts them into organised digital records.

Our goal is simple: **Make digital bookkeeping as easy as having a conversation.**

---

## 🚀 The Problem

Many small shopkeepers still depend on traditional paper-based khata systems to manage customer credits, payments, and daily transactions.

This creates several challenges:

- Maintaining handwritten records takes time.
- Tracking pending customer payments can be difficult.
- Records can get misplaced or become confusing.
- Language and digital literacy barriers make existing accounting apps difficult to use.
- Busy shop environments leave little time for manual data entry.

## 💡 Our Solution

MUNIM introduces a conversational approach to bookkeeping.

Shopkeepers can record transactions using natural voice commands instead of filling out complicated forms.

For example:

> "Ramesh ne aaj 500 rupaye udhaar liye."

MUNIM aims to understand the customer name, amount, transaction type, and date, then organise the information into a digital ledger.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🎙️ **Voice-First Interaction:** Record transactions through natural speech.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support:** Designed for Indian languages and English, including code-mixed conversations.
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Understanding:** Extract customer names, amounts, dates, and transaction details.
- 📒 **Digital Khata:** Maintain organised customer-wise transaction records.
- 💰 **Credit & Payment Tracking:** Keep track of money received and outstanding balances.
- 🗣️ **Voice-Based Corrections:** Allow users to correct misunderstood transaction details conversationally.
- 🔍 **Transaction History:** Review previously recorded transactions.
- ⚡ **Simple Interface:** Designed with small shopkeepers and ease of use in mind.

## 🔄 How MUNIM Works

1. **Speak:** The shopkeeper gives a natural voice command.
2. **Recognise:** Speech recognition converts the voice into text.
3. **Understand:** AI identifies the customer, amount, date, and transaction type.
4. **Confirm:** The shopkeeper can review or correct the extracted information.
5. **Record:** The transaction is saved in the digital ledger.
6. **Track:** Customer balances and transaction history can be accessed easily.
